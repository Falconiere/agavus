# BM25 — retrieval ranking

**Role**: scores query ↔ document relevance over the statute/decision corpus.
The math behind Elasticsearch/Lucene. Future module: `retrieve/search`.

## Formula

$$
\text{score}(q, d) = \sum_{t \in q} \text{idf}(t) \cdot
\frac{\text{tf}(t,d) \cdot (k_1 + 1)}
{\text{tf}(t,d) + k_1 \cdot \left(1 - b + b \cdot \frac{|d|}{\text{avgdl}}\right)}
$$

| Symbol | Meaning | Typical value |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| $\text{tf}(t,d)$ | term frequency in the document | — |
| $\text{idf}(t)$ | rarity weight (same idea as TF-IDF) | — |
| $k_1$ | term-frequency saturation strength | 1.2 |
| $b$ | length-normalization strength | 0.75 |
| $\lvert d \rvert$ / avgdl | document length / average length | — |

## Worked example

Query term "protetiva": $\text{idf} = 3.9$, $\text{tf} = 3$, $|d| = 200$,
avgdl $= 400$, $k_1 = 1.2$, $b = 0.75$.

Length factor: $1 - 0.75 + 0.75 \cdot \frac{200}{400} = 0.625$

$$
\text{score} = 3.9 \cdot \frac{3 \cdot 2.2}{3 + 1.2 \cdot 0.625}
= 3.9 \cdot \frac{6.6}{3.75} = 3.9 \cdot 1.76 \approx 6.86
$$

**Saturation** (same doc, varying tf):

| tf | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| ------- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| factor | 1.26 | 1.76 | 2.05 |

10× the occurrences → only ~1.6× the score. The 10th mention adds little.

**Length normalization** (tf = 3, but a long doc, $|d| = 800$):
factor drops from 1.76 to **1.29**. Long documents don't win by bulk.

## Intuition

TF-IDF's retrieval cousin with two corrections: **diminishing returns** on
repetition, and **fairness across document lengths**. A precise 1-page
decisão can outrank a 50-page acórdão.

## In agavus

- Combined with binary **citation features** ("art. 129", "Lei 11.340") —
  exact statute matches are extremely predictive in legal text.
- Every returned hit must carry a verifiable source reference; answers quote
  retrieved excerpts, never generated text.
